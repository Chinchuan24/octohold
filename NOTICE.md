# Credits 🙏

Octohold stands on two great MIT-licensed skills.

## 🧱 brain-dump by Craig Hewitt
https://github.com/TheCraigHewitt/skills (`cowork/brain-dump`). Copyright (c) 2026 Craig Hewitt.

What Octohold took from it:
- The buckets: projects, tasks, decisions, ideas, concerns, questions
- Saving each dump to a dated file
- The "I can help with…" menu
- The rules: don't invent tasks, don't psychoanalyze

## 👣 adhd-brain-dump by Donna
https://github.com/yuchenbigpen1/donna-skills (`skills/adhd-brain-dump`). Copyright (c) 2026 Yuchen Zhang.

What Octohold took from it:
- One startable step at a time, with the rest held for later
- Feelings never become tasks
- Shrink a step once, then try a different way
- Skip, drop, done and waiting are kept apart
- Handoffs and safety boundaries

## 🐙 New in Octohold
- **Mirror-and-check:** the dump is played back on one screen, and nothing gets planned until you say ✅
- Handles partial yeses and words that voice typing misheard
- Big dumps get confirmed in rounds
- Replies are formatted for ADHD readers
